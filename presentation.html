Introduction

ShareLife is a web application designed to manage blood donor information efficiently.

It helps people find the right donors quickly during emergencies and promotes blood donation for a better and healthier society.

ShareLife – A Blood Donor Management System
Problem Statement
  • Finding blood donors during emergencies is difficult.
  • Donor information is often stored manually.
  • Searching for a matching blood group takes time.
  • There is no centralized system to manage donor records.
